## 🚀 About Me

I am a robotics and computer vision researcher and a **Marie Skłodowska-Curie Doctoral Researcher (DC7) in the European Union-funded WildBotics Doctoral Network at the University of Salamanca, Spain**. I am based in the **TIDOP Research Group**, working at the intersection of autonomous navigation, visual and 3D perception, and artificial intelligence.

My doctoral research focuses on **below-canopy autonomous navigation for environmental sampling**, with the goal of enabling aerial robots to perceive and navigate challenging natural environments. My Master's research explored how Vision-Language Models could guide imitation-learning agents to avoid visual SLAM tracking failures in simulated embodied navigation.

I bring over seven years of professional experience across applied computer vision, 3D mapping and reconstruction, quality assurance, and engineering systems testing. My work has included computer vision pipeline development for automated engineering-drawing analysis, as well as the validation of 3D models and digital twins generated from drone data. I am interested in building reliable, practical systems while pursuing research in autonomous robotics and intelligent perception.
